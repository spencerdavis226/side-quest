# src/components — shared UI

- `Cards.tsx` (`CardFace`, `CardFrame`): live title fit (shrinks to 18px, never truncates), scrollable rules with an overflow affordance, resolved dice result. The pointer handlers deliberately cancel the card's click when a drag or rules scroll happened; a stationary tap must still pass through to the card button. Previous Card and the workshop reuse this component, so a change here changes all three.
- `FullScreenDice.tsx`: portals a transparent full-viewport stage to `document.body`; owns the roll lifecycle (warm while reading, roll, settle 700 ms, fade 180 ms, reveal). It must fall back to the saved values on hidden, resize, Reduced Motion, timeout, or WebGL loss. Keep the lazy `import()`.
- `CardActions.tsx`: secret and timed cards. `useCardActions` holds the hold and timer state for the card in play (reset when the card changes, never saved), `CardActions` renders the Hold to read / Light the fuse / Start the clock / Skip plaques in the `.card-choice` slot (`plaques-1`..`plaques-3` set the columns; Skip calls the ordinary put-aside), and `TimerBlast` portals the BOOM/TIME flash (1.6 s, `pointer-events: none`). Timers run on wall-clock time, so a fuse that ran out in the background goes off on return. `CardFace` takes `shown` (`"secret"` or `"end"`) to swap the rules.
- `UI.tsx`: `Button` focuses itself on click (preserve this; Safari does not focus buttons on tap, and `Modal` restores focus to the opener), `Modal` is a native `<dialog>` with `showModal()` and an exit animation, `IconButton` requires a label.
- `PortraitGate.tsx`: blocks play in phone landscape (user-agent sniff, `orientation: landscape`, and a short side under 600px). iPad landscape plays.
- `PackMarks.tsx`: paints each pack's single-colour `logo` as a CSS mask (gilded in setup and menus, debossed in card footers). A new pack needs only its SVG and `logo` path; no mapping here.
- `Atmosphere.tsx`: decorative looping background; `aria-hidden`, paused when the page is hidden.
- `Crawl.tsx`: everything a crawl shows (styles in `presentation/crawl.css`): `CrawlIcon` (the `public/art/crawl` marks as masks), the hearts meter face (a brass stud like the menu's: hearts left inside a large heart, and a floating −N/+N number when hearts change), `CrawlProgress` (the header's centre plaque: the place in small caps over the floor's room pips, a boss's or ambush's hit pips under its own stage name (Gym 6, Brewery boss), or the omen's or a landing's mark), `CrawlStrip` (the chips under the header, each opening its own dialog, `StripDialog`: the League's type edge on a dice battle or strike (chevrons, opening `MatchupPanel`), the bag (its items and caught Pokémon's marks, or one dim bag when empty), League badges, and a jinx; since 2026-10-31 nothing else), `CardStakes` (one pill per seal in a card's foot, replacing the pack mark in a crawl: risks left (−hearts, a jinx, a boss's or ambush's swings as pips), gains right (+item, +hearts)), `buildExtras`, `UsePlaque` and `ExtrasSheet` (2026-10-27: a card's optional actions, the items and the League's send-ins, are described once as `Extra`s; `extrasLayout` in `extras.ts` puts them on the card's foot only when they fit, one short plaque beside Won and Lost or one or two short ones alone, and otherwise behind one "Use" plaque, "N ready", that opens a sheet with a full-width row each; a send-in counts as long and goes to the sheet beside Won and Lost), `LootPlaque` (Flee, Reroll and Pass it join the card's plaques when `canUseLoot` allows), `CatchPlaques` and `SendInPlaque` (the League's team: throw a ball at a beaten wild Pokémon or let it go; use a caught Pokémon whose type beats the foe: "wins it" on a strike, "roll ×2" on a dice battle, after which the strip's edge chip names it), `FanPlaque` (2026-10-31, owner: never more than three plaques across, `MAX_PLAQUES` and `foldBranches` in `extras.ts`: a branching evolution's forms fold into one Evolve plaque that opens `ExtrasSheet` titled "Evolve into", a row per form with what it changes), `OfferPlaque` (a merchant's ware, a fork's door, a level up or a rest: one or two across, mark beside name with what it does on a line under both; three or four across, stacked; dimmed but solid when it can't be paid), `CrawlRecord` (each bag row has a `DropButton`: Drop or Release turns in place into "Yes" and "Keep", then `onDrop`; hearts in balanced rows, the adventurer or starter, the boons kept (tonight's omen, trinkets), the jinx, the satchel sized by the omen with the draught's Drink button, and the floors on the end screens only; Pub Crawl's end screens show `TrophyWall` and `RunTab` in place of the adventurer and trinket panels) for the party, satchel and jinx dialogs (`part`) and both end screens (everything), and `PartyPulse` (the red or gold flush when hearts change). Powers, knacks, floor rules, the Cup, the Quaff, curses and the Tera Orb were cut 2026-10-31.
- `TopBar.tsx` (2026-10-17 redesign): a brass stud each side and a plaque between. Plain games: the previous-card stud ("Show the last card"), the card count over a deck bar (Round N in an endless game), the menu. Crawls: the hearts stud (opens the party), `CrawlProgress`, the menu, and `CrawlStrip` underneath (`.crawl-deck` adds `--hud-strip` to `--play-chrome`). A meter-mode quest keeps `QuestMeter` on the left.
- `Guide.tsx` (2026-10-17): How to play. Every game's basics; in a crawl its goal and one row per mechanic from `crawlTheme(...).guide`, plus `SealLegend` (the seals on a card, from `Crawl.tsx`). Opened from two places only (owner, 2026-10-17): the pause menu in a game, and "How to play ›" on setup's "How long?" heading, which explains the mode chosen.
- `LeagueMarks.tsx` (2026-10-24): `TypeMark` (a type's medallion), `BadgeCase` (the League's badges dialog and Hall of Fame row) and `badgesLocked` (a `crawlTheme` `badgeLock` card face up shuts the badges chip). Since 2026-10-25 also `TypeEdges` (the team dialog's Strong/Weak against chips), `EdgeMark` (two chevrons, green up or red down: the strip's `tag-edge` chip, owner: an icon, not a banner) and `MatchupPanel` (the chip's "Type matchup" dialog). Types are on the cards (`card.type`); `presentation/pokemon-types.ts` holds their colours and the badge fallback. `CardFace` takes a `matchup` only to show the foe's type in the ribbon; nothing else about the matchup goes on the card.
- `crawlTheme.ts`: a crawl's words and marks per pack (`dungeon`, and `pokemon` for the League's journey: Team HP, Bag, Whited out): item names, the plaque, dialog, setup and end-screen wording, the seal legend, and which `public/art/league` marks replace `art/crawl` ones. Display only; every crawl component takes the theme (`crawlTheme(quest.packId)`). Dungeon's entry is today's text, kept by `tests/browser/crawl.spec.ts`.
- `BallThrow.tsx` (2026-10-31, owner: "much more exciting to watch"): `BallThrowStage` portals the ball throw over a dimmed, blurred table when a ball is thrown live (a new `throwKey` while mounted, never on a resumed game): the ball spins while a three-row slot reel scrolls (one strip of 1 to 20 repeated, one CSS animation to an offset computed from the committed face, so the landing is exact; the result shows on the strip's `animationend`, and the throw is snapshotted so re-renders never restart it), both landing on that face after 2.6 s, then confetti and a gold button for a catch, or the top half flying off and a red flash for one that broke free (1.7 s). Display only; a tap skips it, and Reduced Motion or a hidden tab never plays it. Styles at the end of `presentation/crawl.css`.
- `QuestMeter.tsx`: a quest's meter; a crawl's is the party (hearts, satchel count). `StageBanner.tsx` sweeps once per finale stage, never again for a boss re-dealt or a Champion replaced (a Pub Crawl floor's banner adds its wanted boss); `EvolutionBanner` names an evolution or a level up and what changed, and `PartyNewsBanner` a trinket found, or a jinx laid or lifted, each once and never on a resumed game. Banners are `aria-hidden`; each reads its words through `useAnnounce` (`Announcer.tsx`), which adds a line to the one polite live region rendered from the start in `main.tsx` (a region inserted with its text is often not read).

Buttons should be at least 44px touch targets, labelled, and free of outer focus rings (the project uses a brightness shift). Do not add hover-only affordances; this is a touch app.
