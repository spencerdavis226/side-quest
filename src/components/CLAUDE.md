# src/components — shared UI

- `Cards.tsx` (`CardFace`, `CardFrame`): live title fit (shrinks to 18px, never truncates), scrollable rules with an overflow affordance, resolved dice result. The pointer handlers deliberately cancel the card's click when a drag or rules scroll happened; a stationary tap must still pass through to the card button. Previous Card and the workshop reuse this component, so a change here changes all three.
- `FullScreenDice.tsx`: portals a transparent full-viewport stage to `document.body`; owns the roll lifecycle (warm while reading, roll, settle 700 ms, fade 180 ms, reveal). It must fall back to the saved values on hidden, resize, Reduced Motion, timeout, or WebGL loss. Keep the lazy `import()`.
- `CardActions.tsx`: secret and timed cards. `useCardActions` holds the hold and timer state for the card in play (reset when the card changes, never saved), `CardActions` renders the Hold to read / Light the fuse / Start the clock / Skip plaques in the `.card-choice` slot (`plaques-1`..`plaques-3` set the columns; Skip calls the ordinary put-aside), and `TimerBlast` portals the BOOM/TIME flash (1.6 s, `pointer-events: none`; since 2026-11-01 a shock ring, debris and a shake). `PointCountdown` is Most Likely To's 3-2-1-POINT (a plaque on `likely.*` cards in `Play.tsx`); a lit fuse's spark laps the card (`.fuse-border`), a blown one scorches it, the hold plaque wears a wax seal that breaks. Timers run on wall-clock time, so a fuse that ran out in the background goes off on return. `CardFace` takes `shown` (`"secret"` or `"end"`) to swap the rules.
- `UI.tsx`: `Button` focuses itself on click (preserve this; Safari does not focus buttons on tap, and `Modal` restores focus to the opener), `Modal` is a native `<dialog>` with `showModal()` and an exit animation, `IconButton` requires a label.
- `PortraitGate.tsx`: blocks play in phone landscape (user-agent sniff, `orientation: landscape`, and a short side under 600px). iPad landscape plays.
- `PackMarks.tsx`: paints each pack's single-colour `logo` as a CSS mask (gilded in setup and menus, debossed in card footers). A new pack needs only its SVG and `logo` path; no mapping here.
- `Atmosphere.tsx`: decorative looping background; `aria-hidden`, paused when the page is hidden.
- `Crawl.tsx`: everything a crawl shows (styles in `presentation/crawl.css`): `CrawlIcon` (the `public/art/crawl` marks as masks), `useHeartPulse` (the hearts' change, for the HUD's gauge and splat), `CardStakes` (one pill per seal in a card's foot, replacing the pack mark in a crawl: risks left (−hearts, a jinx, a boss's or ambush's swings as pips), gains right (+item, +hearts)), `buildExtras`, `UsePlaque` and `ExtrasSheet` (2026-10-27: a card's optional actions, the items and the League's send-ins, are described once as `Extra`s; `extrasLayout` in `extras.ts` puts them on the card's foot only when they fit, one short plaque beside Won and Lost or one or two short ones alone, and otherwise behind one "Use" plaque, "N ready", that opens a sheet with a full-width row each; a send-in counts as long and goes to the sheet beside Won and Lost), `LootPlaque` (Flee, Reroll and Pass it join the card's plaques when `canUseLoot` allows), `CatchPlaques` and `SendInPlaque` (the League's team: throw a ball at a beaten wild Pokémon or let it go; use a caught Pokémon whose type beats the foe: "wins it" on a strike, "roll ×2" on a dice battle, after which the strip's edge chip names it), `FanPlaque` (2026-10-31, owner: never more than three plaques across, `MAX_PLAQUES` and `foldBranches` in `extras.ts`: a branching evolution's forms fold into one Evolve plaque that opens `ExtrasSheet` titled "Evolve into", a row per form with what it changes), `OfferPlaque` (a merchant's ware, a fork's door, a level up or a rest: one or two across, mark beside name with what it does on a line under both; three or four across, stacked; dimmed but solid when it can't be paid), `CrawlRecord` (each bag row has a `DropButton`: Drop or Release turns in place into "Yes" and "Keep", then `onDrop`; hearts in balanced rows, the adventurer or starter, the boons kept (tonight's omen, trinkets), the jinx, the satchel sized by the omen with the draught's Drink button, and the floors on the end screens only; Pub Crawl's end screens show `TrophyWall` and `RunTab` in place of the adventurer and trinket panels) for the party, satchel and jinx dialogs (`part`) and both end screens (everything), and `PartyPulse` (the red or gold flush when hearts change). Powers, knacks, floor rules, the Cup, the Quaff, curses and the Tera Orb were cut 2026-10-31.
- The HUD (2026-11-16, rebuilt per mode; owner: "much simpler and more elegant ... curses and such don't need names, just an icon"): a crawl's header is `hud/LeagueHud.tsx` or `hud/QuestHud.tsx` (`TopBar` hands over), drawn from `hud/model.ts` (`hudPlace`, `hudStatus`; spoken labels as the old header had them) with `hud/parts.tsx` (`StatusButton`, `HeartSplat`, `Track`); styles `presentation/hud-league.css` and `hud-quest.css` (eager). One bar (hearts gauge, place, menu) and one status line of marks and numbers (`.hud-strip .hud-stat`, `tag-*`); alerts (`hud-alert`: type edge, sent-in helper, curse) are marks alone. Fly targets: `hearts` on the gauge, `party`, `satchel`, `badges`, `lore`, `rival`, `matchup`, `jinx`. The old `CrawlStrip`, `CrawlProgress` and the stud face are gone.
- The tray and menus (2026-11-16): `trayTabs.ts` (eager) lists a run's pages, titled with the old dialogs' names. `tray/` is lazy (warmed by `warmTray`). `Sheet.tsx` is the iOS-style sheet both use: a modal `<dialog>`, no close button (the full-width grabber is the button named Close), pulled down from anywhere and from content marked `data-sheet-scroll` when it is at its top (`useSheetDrag` in `gestures.ts`: touch events for touch, so it can stop the native scroll; pointer events for a mouse or pen). `Tray.tsx` adds the pager (`usePager`) and dock; `Menu.tsx` is a crawl's pause menu and End game question (plain games keep `GameDialogs`' dialog); `LeagueTray.tsx`/`QuestTray.tsx` the pages; `Inventory.tsx` the item grid and inspector; `spring.ts` the `linear()` springs. Styles `presentation/tray.css`, `tray-league.css`, `tray-quest.css` (lazy). Pages one away from the shown one are drawn but `inert`; tests scope to `.tray-page.shown`. Performance rules (2026-11-16, owner on an iPhone: "choppy and low fps"): a gesture frame writes `transform`/`opacity` on the moving element only, never an inherited custom property on the dialog, reads sizes once per gesture, and batches to one write per frame; pages are built once and memoized, far pages `content-visibility: hidden`; no looping animation of `filter`, `box-shadow`, `clip-path` or layout properties in the tray or HUD; no blurred shadows, filters or repeating gradients in the tray skins (they stalled the open in WebKit). The rise is stepped per frame from `sheetAt`, never by the clock, so an open stall cannot skip it (owner, 2026-11-16: "snap up to like the middle"). The sheet is anchored by `top`/`bottom` in the dialog's own box, never sized from `--app-height` (the installed app's fixed box is an inset shorter: the Dynamic Island bug).
- `TopBar.tsx` (2026-10-17 redesign): a brass stud each side and a plaque between. Plain games: the previous-card stud ("Show the last card"), the card count over a deck bar (Round N in an endless game), the menu. Crawls hand the whole header to their mode's HUD (`hud/`, 2026-11-16; `.crawl-deck` adds `--hud-strip` to `--play-chrome`). A meter-mode quest keeps `QuestMeter` on the left.
- `Guide.tsx` (2026-10-17): How to play. Every game's basics; in a crawl its goal and one row per mechanic from `crawlTheme(...).guide`, plus `SealLegend` (the seals on a card, from `Crawl.tsx`). Opened from two places only (owner, 2026-10-17): the pause menu in a game, and "How to play ›" on setup's "How long?" heading, which explains the mode chosen.
- `LeagueMarks.tsx` (2026-10-24): `TypeMark` (a type's medallion), `BadgeCase` (the League's badges dialog and Hall of Fame row) and `badgesLocked` (a `crawlTheme` `badgeLock` card face up shuts the badges chip). Since 2026-10-25 also `TypeEdges` (the team dialog's Strong/Weak against chips), `EdgeMark` (two chevrons, green up or red down: the strip's `tag-edge` chip, owner: an icon, not a banner) and `MatchupPanel` (the chip's "Type matchup" dialog). Types are on the cards (`card.type`); `presentation/pokemon-types.ts` holds their colours and the badge fallback. `CardFace` takes a `matchup` only to show the foe's type in the ribbon; nothing else about the matchup goes on the card.
- `crawlTheme.ts`: a crawl's words and marks per pack (`dungeon`, and `pokemon` for the League's journey: Team HP, Bag, Whited out): item names, the plaque, dialog, setup and end-screen wording, the seal legend, and which `public/art/league` marks replace `art/crawl` ones. Display only; every crawl component takes the theme (`crawlTheme(quest.packId)`). Dungeon's entry is today's text, kept by `tests/browser/crawl.spec.ts`.
- `BallThrow.tsx` (2026-10-31, owner: "much more exciting to watch"; one look only, the donut, owner: "default donut, no other option"): `BallThrowStage` portals the ball throw over a dimmed, blurred table when a ball is thrown live (a new `throwKey` while mounted, never on a resumed game). `DonutBall` is a hollow ring (red over white, black band) with one number in its hole: one eased value turns the ring a whole number of turns and picks the number from a strip shuffled every throw (never sequential, never the same number twice in a row), so the number slows with the ball and settles on the engine's committed face at 82% of the 2.6 s (`COUNT_DONE`), while the ball is still visibly turning, then the ball finishes with the face steady (counting to the very end made the face appear after the ball had all but stopped, which read as stopping on one number and showing another); per-frame work sets DOM styles directly. The throw is snapshotted when it starts, because `thrownBall()` returns a new object every render and a dependency on it restarted the timers. Then confetti and a gold glow for a catch, or the top half flying off and a red flash for one that broke free (1.7 s). Display only; a tap skips it, and Reduced Motion or a hidden tab never plays it. Styles at the end of `presentation/crawl.css`.
- `Flourish.tsx` (2026-10-31; League and every-mode parts 2026-11-01): `NaturalBurst` portals a gold (natural 20) or red (natural 1) flash when a d20 lands on a natural live in any mode (never on a resumed result or an undo back onto it). `FelledStamp` is the stamp over the card's whole text area on a boss's felling swing (`fellingSwing`) or felling Won (`fellingVerdict`; `Play.tsx` holds the settle `STAMP_MS`), worded by `crawlTheme(...).flourish` (Felled; the League's Badge won / Defeated). `useBossReturn` + `BossTally`: a boss or ambush dealt again after a landed hit jolts and shows its hit tally for 1.8 s. `Atmosphere` takes `scene` (`crawlScene`, Pub Crawl only): `data-scene` recolours the backdrop per floor through CSS variables in `theme.css`. - `QuestMeter.tsx`: a quest's meter; a crawl's is the party (hearts, satchel count). `StageBanner.tsx` sweeps once per finale stage, never again for a boss re-dealt or a Champion replaced (a Pub Crawl floor's banner adds its wanted boss); `EvolutionBanner` names an evolution or a level up and what changed, and `PartyNewsBanner` a lost fight's drinks passed on (the Bar Tab; a trinket, a jinx, a hatch and a faint are moments now), each once and never on a resumed game. Banners are `aria-hidden`; each reads its words through `useAnnounce` (`Announcer.tsx`), which adds a line to the one polite live region rendered from the start in `main.tsx` (a region inserted with its text is often not read).

- `ChoiceSheet.tsx` (2026-11-15): a card whose offers do something is chosen from a sheet (`choosesInSheet`): one `ChoicePlaque` at the card's foot opens `ChoiceSheet`, a row per way (`choiceRows`: name, ♥ and drink price, what it brings in words, a note, or why it can't be taken). Plain pairs (`plainOffers`) and League catches keep `OfferPlaque`s. New offer fields need a sentence in `choiceRows`.

Buttons should be at least 44px touch targets, labelled, and free of outer focus rings (the project uses a brightness shift). Do not add hover-only affordances; this is a touch app.
