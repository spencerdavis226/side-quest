# Side Quest — game and content brief

For the intended humor, specificity, and card-writing personality, also use [Card voice and theme reference](CARD_VOICE_REFERENCE.md). It distills the reusable creative direction from Spencer's earlier CABIIN card set without importing that game's board, team, zone, or movement rules.

## Pillars

A shared deck, a lively table, one clear instruction at a time. A reveal should be delightful and a rule should make sense on one reading. No accounts, scorekeeping, or player setup. The app deals cards; the people handle the activity. The one exception to "the app only deals" is Secrets & fuses (owner decision, 2026-10-06): a card may hold a secret one player reads by holding a plaque, or a timer (a hidden fuse or a visible countdown) that keeps the card on the table until time is up. Neither is saved or tracked between cards.

## Core editorial direction

Classic party: short drinking prompts, broad group prompts, categories, playful verbal challenges, temporary rules, and dice gambles. Instructions use everyday language. Pours are printed on the card or resolved from a committed dice roll; the app never enforces a pour, and any drink can be nonalcoholic. The House deck ships exactly as supplied: blunt, specific, and without consent boilerplate.

Choose anyone to draw first. Pass the device clockwise unless a card names the next drawer. The drawer starts a category or challenge. Complete its activity before dismissing the card. Category rounds end on the first repeat or blank; no stopwatch or debate about how long someone hesitated. Every card makes its actor explicit: the drawing player, the whole table, or a named subset. A lasting rule runs either for the rest of the game or until the drawing player's next turn — never until the next card — with explicit consequences in the card text. No rule requires app bookkeeping.

## Current content

Seven opt-in packs. `core` (105 cards) is the generated main deck: the supplied sample set (31), trimmed classics (45), and the trimmed dice expansion (29). `house` (94 cards) is the supplied Sheet1 restored verbatim in `src/content/custom.ts`; its 8 CABIIN rows play in Cabin weekend. `VIP night` (16 cards) covers birthdays and celebrations. `pokemon` (120 cards, `src/content/pokemon.ts`) translates the supplied Kanto/Johto/Hoenn board sheets into the shared-deck format and has its own mode, Pokémon League, beside Short/Long/Infinite (it holds the Pokémon pack on and other packs can join; the mode adds the 24 gym leaders of Kanto, Johto and Hoenn, which never appear in the plain modes; each is a hard battle marked with a gold ribbon naming its badge and earns a badge on a meter in the top row win or lose; the eighth badge starts the gauntlet, picked at random when the game starts: one Legendary (of 8), four of the Elite Four (of 9), then a Champion (of 3, the whole table fights), each with its own ribbon; beating the Champion ends the game; eight leaders are spread through the first 40 draws, so a run is about 30 to 40 cards). In translating: gyms became leader battles, board movement and lost turns became pours or one-shot dice, and untitled spaces are named after the Pokémon in them. `cabin` (`Cabin weekend`, `src/content/cabin.ts`) translates the CABIIN 2.0 board game in `reference/cabiin-2/`: 85 cards, including the 11 CABIIN-born cards that used to sit in Core and the 8 CABIIN rows of the house sheet (verbatim), all under `cabin.*` IDs; the old `core.*` and `house.sheet-*` IDs are retired. No card is in two packs. Teams became the drawing player or a home-state call-out; board-only spaces, guess-the-roll spaces, spaces that depend on another card, and lore that needs its backstory were left out. `likely` (`Most Likely To`, `src/content/likely.ts`) is 250 standalone vote cards in the spirit of Drunk Stoned or Stupid: on three everyone points, and the most-voted player drinks (2 by default; some prompts pay 3, a shot, or a finished drink). Nothing is tallied between cards. `secrets` (`Secrets & fuses`, `src/content/secrets.ts`) is 150 cards built on the hold-to-read and timer mechanics: 75 secrets (Paranoia, secret missions, Fact or Cap, truth-or-lie stories, secret rules), 63 on a visible clock (16 Forehead, 16 No Saying It, 10 Act It Out, 10 Three in Five and 11 timed challenges) and 12 fuses. A fuse never sends the phone round (owner, 2026-10-06: no broken phones): the phone stays on the table while a beer is passed, and whoever holds the beer at the boom pays. Mechanics are borrowed from Paranoia, Killer, Heads Up, Taboo, charades, 5 Second Rule and hot potato; every prompt is original. Source consequences are kept as written — shots, chugs, gendered splits, mixed drinks and rough dares are not softened. Any combination of packs can be selected; at least one is required. The 670 cards of the plain modes comprise 93 sip, 326 group, 30 category, 152 challenge, and 69 rule cards, 95 of them dice cards; Pokémon League adds the 24 gym leaders (12 with dice) and the 20 gauntlet cards. This distribution is an editorial starting point, not validated balance.

The main deck was trimmed hard on 2026-09-26: near-identical "roll d6, then one of two things" branches, duplicate call-outs and categories, and flavorless filler were deleted. Every remaining dice card resolves to one exact computed instruction (odds/evens, doubles, per-die amounts included) before it is read aloud. All cards are equally likely per shuffle cycle; a deck longer than the pool repeats cards only after exhausting it, and the cycle boundary never repeats immediately when the pool has multiple cards.

The supplied sheet is the authority for the House deck. `src/content/custom.ts` uses its 1-based row numbers in stable IDs (`house.sheet-003` through `house.sheet-105`, excluding blank row 75 and the description-less row 106; `vip.sheet-003` through `vip.sheet-006`). Titles, jokes, threats, and pours are transcribed as written, including the sheet's own typos in titles, with no permission-seeking or softened consequences. The 2026-09-26 logic pass made minimal clarity edits only where the sheet was ambiguous about who acts or how long a lasting rule lasts (`house.sheet-005`, `house.sheet-034`, `house.sheet-082`, `house.sheet-099`, and rows 40 and 53, now `cabin.first-name-only` and `cabin.abra-like-a-slut`); everything else stays verbatim. The eight rows that are CABIIN board spaces (sheet rows 12, 16, 24, 28, 40, 42, 53 and 54) play in Cabin weekend instead, verbatim, as `cabin.maddy-booty`, `cabin.ursaring`, `cabin.wench`, `cabin.no-take-give`, `cabin.first-name-only`, `cabin.pet-that-dog`, `cabin.abra-like-a-slut` and `cabin.whinnie-the-pooh` (owner decision, 2026-10-05). Rows that name dice keep the app roll and compute the pour. Repeated headings such as Categories and Call Out remain separate cards because their prompts differ.

Keep one primary instruction. Aim for 90 characters and 18 words; the build flags longer rules and rejects those over 120 characters or 24 words (long-word titles are rejected over 15 characters). State who acts — the drawing player, everyone, or a named subset — and how long a lasting rule lasts: the rest of the game or until the drawing player's next turn, never until the next card. Specify who starts and when an activity stops where relevant. Preserve stable IDs for unchanged concepts. Text revisions affect new sessions only; active sessions keep their snapshot.

## Review gate

Use the workshop to inspect every card at 320px and enlarged text. Play a Short (30) game with a real group, then sample more cards across several sessions. Use automated checks for the full 670-card shuffle cycle; a human need not draw 500 cards to review the deck. Record explanations, unclear endings, and repetition before changing the collection. Do not introduce weights or pacing rules without observed need. Lock copy and IDs before any further content work.
