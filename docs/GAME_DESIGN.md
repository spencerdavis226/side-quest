# Side Quest — game and content brief

For the intended humor, specificity, and card-writing personality, also use [Card voice and theme reference](CARD_VOICE_REFERENCE.md). It distills the reusable creative direction from Spencer's earlier CABIIN card set without importing that game's board, team, zone, or movement rules.

## Pillars

A shared deck, a lively table, one clear instruction at a time. A reveal should be delightful and a rule should make sense on one reading. No accounts, scorekeeping, or player setup. The app deals cards; the people handle the activity. The one exception to "the app only deals" is Secrets & fuses (owner decision, 2026-10-06): a card may hold a secret one player reads by holding a plaque, or a timer (a hidden fuse or a visible countdown) that keeps the card on the table until time is up. Neither is saved or tracked between cards.

## Core editorial direction

Classic party: short drinking prompts, broad group prompts, categories, playful verbal challenges, temporary rules, and dice gambles. Instructions use everyday language. Pours are printed on the card or resolved from a committed dice roll; the app never enforces a pour, and any drink can be nonalcoholic. The House deck ships exactly as supplied: blunt, specific, and without consent boilerplate.

Choose anyone to draw first. Pass the device clockwise unless a card names the next drawer. The drawer starts a category or challenge. Complete its activity before dismissing the card. Category rounds end on the first repeat or blank; no stopwatch or debate about how long someone hesitated. Every card makes its actor explicit: the drawing player, the whole table, or a named subset. A lasting rule runs either for the rest of the game or until the drawing player's next turn — never until the next card — with explicit consequences in the card text. No rule requires app bookkeeping.

## Current content

Eight opt-in packs. `core` (96 cards) is the generated main deck: the sample set (25), classics and King's Cup ranks (24), and the standard expansion (47), rewritten on 2026-10-07 to be cruder and to replace bare roll-and-pour cards with table moments. `house` (94 cards) is the supplied Sheet1 restored verbatim in `src/content/custom.ts`; its 8 CABIIN rows play in Cabin weekend. `VIP night` (16 cards) covers birthdays and celebrations. `pokemon` (120 cards, `src/content/pokemon.ts`) translates the supplied Kanto/Johto/Hoenn board sheets into the shared-deck format and has its own mode, Pokémon League, beside Short/Long/Infinite. Since 2026-10-16 (owner: "the dungeon crawl is drastically more exciting") the League is a journey on the Pub Crawl engine and plays the Pokémon pack alone; the 120 deck cards play only in the plain modes. The team shares 17 HP (Pub Crawl's hearts, shown as Poké Balls). A run opens on a starter (1 of 17, from every generation), whose trade-off holds for the run: Charmander's wild fights hurt one less but bosses one more, Squirtle starts with a Protect but a bag of two, Pikachu has two more HP and an X Attack and refuses to evolve. Five of the eight routes carry an evolution card (a Rare Candy, an evolution stone, "What? It's evolving!"): evolve for a drink and the starter's next form replaces its effect with a stronger one (Charmeleon, then Charizard), or press B for one HP; a fully evolved starter can only press B. Eevee branches: its Evolve plaque splits into Vaporeon, Jolteon and Flareon, the table picks one, and that is its last form. About three runs in four evolve at least once and a third reach the final form. Then eight routes of four cards from each route's fifteen (wild Pokémon, trainers, hazards such as Zubat caves and Snorlax, catches (a drink for a ball, and HP back), items, a Pokémon Center (+2 HP), a Poké Mart, and a crossroads whose loud path deals one of the route's two Ace Trainers), each closed by a gym: gym k is one of nine leaders, every region's kth gym from Kanto to Paldea (Alola's Trial Captains and Kahunas stand in), so the lineup is random but still ramps up. After the fourth gym the run detours through one of nine villain hideouts (Rocket, Magma, Aqua, Galactic, Plasma, Flare, Skull, Macro Cosmos, Star: two grunts, then a boss beaten twice), then Victory Road, the rival (beaten twice), a Legendary (1 of 30, two hits), four of 36 Elite Four members (Galar's Champion Cup finalists stand in) and a Champion (1 of 13, three hits, the whole table fights). Items: Potion (down a fresh drink for one HP), Protect (blocks the next loss), X Attack (roll a lost battle again), Escape Rope (escape a wild or trainer battle, never a gym). Statuses: Poisoned (each loss costs one more), Confused (the bag is sealed), until the next gym falls. Zero HP whites the team out and everyone finishes their drink; beating the Champion is the Hall of Fame. Badges fill a row in the team dialog. `tests/league.test.ts` plays 4,000 seeded runs: about 23% reach the Hall of Fame (14% with Piplup to 42% with Mudkip; every hideout 20% to 25%), a won run takes about 68 draws (64 to 73), and most whiteouts come at the Elite Four, the Champion and the Legendary. Old saves from the meter-mode League still play to their end. In translating: gyms became leader battles, board movement and lost turns became pours or one-shot dice, and untitled spaces are named after the Pokémon in them. `cabin` (`Cabin weekend`, `src/content/cabin.ts`) translates the CABIIN 2.0 board game in `reference/cabiin-2/`: 85 cards, including the 11 CABIIN-born cards that used to sit in Core and the 8 CABIIN rows of the house sheet (verbatim), all under `cabin.*` IDs; the old `core.*` and `house.sheet-*` IDs are retired. No card is in two packs. Teams became the drawing player or a home-state call-out; board-only spaces, guess-the-roll spaces, spaces that depend on another card, and lore that needs its backstory were left out. `likely` (`Most Likely To`, `src/content/likely.ts`) is 250 standalone vote cards in the spirit of Drunk Stoned or Stupid: on three everyone points, and the most-voted player drinks (2 by default; some prompts pay 3, a shot, or a finished drink). Nothing is tallied between cards. `secrets` (`Secrets & fuses`, `src/content/secrets.ts`) is 150 cards built on the hold-to-read and timer mechanics: 75 secrets (Paranoia, secret missions, Fact or Cap, truth-or-lie stories, secret rules), 63 on a visible clock (16 Forehead, 16 No Saying It, 10 Act It Out, 10 Three in Five and 11 timed challenges) and 12 fuses. A fuse never sends the phone round (owner, 2026-10-06: no broken phones): the phone stays on the table while a beer is passed, and whoever holds the beer at the boom pays. Mechanics are borrowed from Paranoia, Killer, Heads Up, Taboo, charades, 5 Second Rule and hot potato; every prompt is original. Source consequences are kept as written — shots, chugs, gendered splits, mixed drinks and rough dares are not softened. `dungeon` (`Dungeon night`, `src/content/dungeon.ts`, 2026-10-15): the bar has a cellar, and the cellar goes down forever. In the plain modes its 118 monsters, elites, ambushes, trials, traps and bosses play as ordinary cards (a lost fight costs a drink). Its own mode, Pub Crawl, plays the pack alone as a run. The run opens on tonight's omen (1 of 12): a rule for the whole crawl with a trade-off, such as Flooded Stairs (traps cost a heart more, but a smoke bomb to start), The Lock-In (bosses take a hit more, but a loaded die), Cargo Shorts (a satchel of four), or a table rule the app doesn't track (Lefty Night). Then come three floors, each one of two (the Cellar or the Wine Cellar, the Catacombs or the Sewers, the Drowned Taproom or the Kitchen: eight different dungeons), of seven rooms picked at random from the floor's 21, and a boss at the end of each (one of three; 1 and 2 hits), with Last Call (one of four, 3 hits) after the third floor. A boss is dealt again to the next drawer until it falls. The table shares 12 hearts: a lost fight costs the drawer a drink and the party hearts (shown as a seal on the card, never in the rules), and at zero the party wipes and everyone finishes their drink. Room fights get harder each floor (d20 6+, 8+, 10+; traps 8+, 10+, 12+; bosses 8+, 10+, 11+). Each floor also holds an ambush (two of them: beaten twice, one swing per deal), a merchant (an item for a drink price, or for a heart, or walk on), and a fork (the quiet door, or the loud door, which turns the floor's next room into one of its two elites: three harder, a heart dearer, and always an item on a win). Finds, merchants, forks and omens are Pub Crawl only: treasure, shrines (+2 hearts) and relics (walk away or roll; a win adds an item, a loss costs a heart and sometimes a curse). The satchel holds three items (unless an omen says otherwise): a Healing Draught (down a fresh, full drink for one heart; healing is meant to be hard, owner 2026-10-15), a Coaster Shield (blocks the next heart loss), a Loaded Die (roll a lost fight again) and a Smoke Bomb (escape a room fight, never a boss or an ambush). A curse (Bleeding: each heart loss costs one more; Hexed: the satchel is sealed) lasts until the floor's boss falls; some bosses curse on a missed swing. Inspired by Heroes of Barcadia's drink-as-damage fights; every card is original. `tests/dungeon.test.ts` plays 4,000 seeded runs through the real engine with a sensible table: about 28% of runs survive (from 17% on The Lock-In to 36% on The Regulars; 22% to 30% by route), and most wipes come on the last two floors.

Any combination of packs can be selected; at least one is required. Pub Crawl and Pokémon League play their own pack alone; the other selections are kept for the next game. The 929 cards of the plain modes comprise 92 sip, 377 group, 49 category, 339 challenge, and 72 rule cards, 175 of them dice cards; Pokémon League deals none of them: it plays its own 356 League-only cards (72 gym leaders, 30 Legendaries, 36 Elite Four, 13 Champions, 17 starters, 152 route, Victory Road and rival cards, 36 hideout cards). This distribution is an editorial starting point, not validated balance.

The 2026-10-07 rewrite (owner request: "more creative and crude-funny", all decks) cut Core's bare "roll, then drink or give a number" cards and their duplicates (Core went from 36 dice cards to 16) and added cards where something happens between people. A dice card now states a premise and lets each result carry a punchline with its pour; rules text no longer spells out the outcome table. Across the other packs the same pass reworded math-first dice rules (Pokémon, League, Cabin), punched up flat House rows and the generated VIP cards, and swapped 61 bland Most Likely To prompts for cruder ones. The main deck was trimmed hard on 2026-09-26: near-identical "roll d6, then one of two things" branches, duplicate call-outs and categories, and flavorless filler were deleted. Every remaining dice card resolves to one exact computed instruction (odds/evens, doubles, per-die amounts included) before it is read aloud. All cards are equally likely per shuffle cycle; a deck longer than the pool repeats cards only after exhausting it, and the cycle boundary never repeats immediately when the pool has multiple cards.

The supplied sheet is the authority for the House deck. `src/content/custom.ts` uses its 1-based row numbers in stable IDs (`house.sheet-003` through `house.sheet-105`, excluding blank row 75 and the description-less row 106; `vip.sheet-003` through `vip.sheet-006`). Titles, jokes, threats, and pours are transcribed as written, including the sheet's own typos in titles, with no permission-seeking or softened consequences. The 2026-09-26 logic pass made minimal clarity edits only where the sheet was ambiguous about who acts or how long a lasting rule lasts (`house.sheet-005`, `house.sheet-034`, `house.sheet-082`, `house.sheet-099`, and rows 40 and 53, now `cabin.first-name-only` and `cabin.abra-like-a-slut`); everything else stays verbatim. The eight rows that are CABIIN board spaces (sheet rows 12, 16, 24, 28, 40, 42, 53 and 54) play in Cabin weekend instead, verbatim, as `cabin.maddy-booty`, `cabin.ursaring`, `cabin.wench`, `cabin.no-take-give`, `cabin.first-name-only`, `cabin.pet-that-dog`, `cabin.abra-like-a-slut` and `cabin.whinnie-the-pooh` (owner decision, 2026-10-05). Rows that name dice keep the app roll and compute the pour. Repeated headings such as Categories and Call Out remain separate cards because their prompts differ.

Keep one primary instruction. Aim for 90 characters and 18 words; the build flags longer rules and rejects those over 120 characters or 24 words (long-word titles are rejected over 15 characters). State who acts — the drawing player, everyone, or a named subset — and how long a lasting rule lasts: the rest of the game or until the drawing player's next turn, never until the next card. Specify who starts and when an activity stops where relevant. Preserve stable IDs for unchanged concepts. Text revisions affect new sessions only; active sessions keep their snapshot.

## Review gate

Use the workshop to inspect every card at 320px and enlarged text. Play a Short (30) game with a real group, then sample more cards across several sessions. Use automated checks for the full 929-card shuffle cycle; a human need not draw 500 cards to review the deck. Record explanations, unclear endings, and repetition before changing the collection. Do not introduce weights or pacing rules without observed need. Lock copy and IDs before any further content work.
